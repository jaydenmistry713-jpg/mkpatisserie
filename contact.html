Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Services

How far in advance should I book?

▼

For catering and event services, we recommend booking at least 3 weeks in advance. Custom cakes require 2 weeks notice, while cupcakes and other bakery items need 5 days. Workshop bookings should be made as soon as dates are announced on our Instagram.

Do you accommodate dietary requirements?

▼

Yes! We can accommodate most dietary requirements including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and specific allergies. Please inform us of any requirements when placing your order so we can ensure everything is prepared safely.

What areas do you serve?

▼

We're based in Leicester and offer collection and delivery within Leicester. For catering and events, we travel nationwide. Delivery for bakery items can be arranged within Leicester and surrounding areas, with nationwide delivery available for special occasions.

Can I see examples of your previous work?

▼

Absolutely! Check out our Gallery to see photos of our cakes, catering setups, and events. You can also follow us on Instagram @mkpatisserieltd for regular updates and behind-the-scenes content.

Do you offer event styling services?

▼

Yes! We offer complete event styling packages including balloon arches, backdrops, table arrangements, centerpieces, and themed décor. This can be added to any catering package or booked as a standalone service.

What payment methods do you accept?

▼

We accept bank transfers, cash, and card payments. For larger catering and event bookings, we typically require a deposit to secure your date, with the balance due before the event.

How do I place an order?

▼

You can place orders through the booking forms on our Catering and Bakery pages, or contact us directly via email, phone, or Instagram DM. We'll get back to you within 24-48 hours to confirm details and finalize your order.
