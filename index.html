About MK

Our Story

Since 2019, MK Patisserie has evolved from a small cake studio into a premier event planning and catering company. Born from a genuine love for creating beautiful, memorable flavours, we've grown to offer everything from bespoke desserts to complete event experiences. Each project reflects our commitment to refined aesthetics, seamless execution, and the art of bringing people together through moments that feel truly elevated and unforgettable.
