About MK

Our Story & Values

Our Journey

Founded in 2019, MK began as a small cake studio built on a love for creating beautiful, thoughtful flavours. What started with bespoke cakes soon grew into a wider world of desserts and baked goods — each year opening the door to something new.

That steady evolution became part of the brand's DNA. As demand grew, so did the vision: from catering, to table styling, to full event experiences. Today, MK has grown into a modern event-planning and catering company, known for pairing refined aesthetics with seamless execution.

What ties it all together is simple — a genuine passion for hosting, creating atmosphere, and bringing people together. Every project is crafted with care, intention, and a commitment to making moments feel elevated and unforgettable.
