Business Information & Policies

  • Payment Methods: Deposits can be made via PayPal or Bank transfer

Cake Orders & Collection

▼

Collection Instructions:

  • When collecting a cake, please hold the box from the bottom with both hands
  • Place the cake on a flat surface in the car (preferably by the passengers footwell)
  • Do NOT place the cake on the seat as it is more likely to collapse

Storage & Shelf Life:

  • Cakes are baked fresh and will last 3-4 days, 1 week in the fridge, or 2 months frozen
  • If cakes contain fresh fruits, please consume within the same day
  • Cakes can be kept in room temperature if cut on the same day
  • Fresh cream cakes need to be kept in the fridge if not cut within 4 hours from collecting the cake

Design & Color Matching:

  • When working with a certain color palette for a cake, I will try my best to match it as close as possible. There may be a difference in color.
  • If you give me a design to recreate for you, I will match it as close as I can, but will never copy another baker's design exactly, as all my cakes are unique and different.
  • We cannot guarantee the exact same design or color when replicating another cake or even my own design.

Customer Responsibility:

  • It is the customer's responsibility to check the cake when collecting it. Once it leaves my premises I will not take responsibility for any damages or dislikes of the cake.

Cake Jars

▼

Storage:

  • Cake jars containing fruits or fresh cream need to be consumed on the same day or kept in the fridge straight away and consumed within 3-4 days
  • For any other flavors there's a shelf life of 3 days or kept in the fridge for 1 week

Postage:

  • Jars that contain fruits and fresh cream are not suitable for postage orders
  • All other flavors can be posted anywhere in the UK via Royal mail 1st class or special delivery

Ordering:

  • For a selection of flavors I usually take orders for these on specific Cake jar days. Keep an eye out for my stories or posts.
  • For any custom orders on my cake jars there's a minimum order of 4 jars per flavor

Postal Orders

▼

Delivery Options:

  • We have 2 options when posting any orders: 1st class signed for and recorded delivery via Royal mail
  • For 1st class signed for they will require your signature so please make sure someone is at home to receive your post

Liability:

  • We do not take any liability for losses or damages done via Royal Mail. Therefore, I will not be issuing any refunds for mistakes made on their part such as loss or mishandling.
  • However for recorded delivery, I will be able to track the parcel and make sure it does not get lost. If in any case the parcel does get lost, I can refund the postal fee, BUT not the full refund for the product/goods.

Important:

  • Please make sure you choose the correct postage option when placing your order

General Product Information

▼

Product Details:

  • When placing an order with me please give me as much detail as possible for the product/s you require
  • When it comes to chocolate covered strawberries, please note that they are made with fresh, natural fruit. Because strawberries are grown in nature and not manufactured, we cannot guarantee uniform size, appearance, or taste. Variations are normal and part of what makes each strawberry unique
  • I would really appreciate it if you could avoid requesting photos before collection, as it can be a bit difficult for me to keep up with all the requests. Where possible, I will of course share photos. Thank you for your understanding

Cancellations & Refunds:

  • 50% non-refundable deposit is required within 24 hrs when placing an order
  • Deposits will not be refunded if you decide to cancel a cake, however under extreme circumstances I will be happy to put that towards your next order within the year
  • If I cancel your order due to any unforeseen circumstances, I will refund the deposit
